# FinCEN BSA/AML Regulatory Reference

## Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) Statutory Obligations
- **Currency Transaction Report (CTR)**: Governed by 31 CFR § 1010.311. Financial institutions must file FinCEN Form 112 for each transaction in currency of more than $10,000.
- **Structuring Prohibition**: Governed by 31 U.S.C. § 5324. Prohibits conducting transactions in currency of less than $10,000 for the purpose of evading the CTR reporting requirements.
